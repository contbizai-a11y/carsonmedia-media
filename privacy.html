Privacy policy

carsinmedia publisher · last updated 7 November 2026

What the app is

The carsinmedia publisher is a private tool. It uploads the car-news photo posts of @carsinmedia to the @carsinmedia TikTok account. Only the owner of that account signs in to it. It is not offered to other users.

What we receive from TikTok

  • The access token and refresh token for the @carsinmedia account, which let the tool upload posts.
  • Basic account information (the account's ID and display name), used only to confirm that the right account is connected.
  • The status of each upload, used to check that it arrived.

How we use and store it

The tokens are kept as encrypted secrets in a private repository and are used only to upload our own posts. We do not collect data from any other TikTok user. We do not sell, share or analyse any data, and this site sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep the tokens only while the tool is connected. Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. We then delete the stored tokens.
